2. What happens when you visit

When you open this website or a badge (for example embedded in a README on GitHub), our hosting provider processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested URL, referrer and user agent. This is required to deliver the content and to protect the service against abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and working service (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Badges embedded in GitHub READMEs are usually fetched through GitHub's image proxy. In that case we receive GitHub's IP address, not the reader's.

3. Hosting by Cloudflare

The website and the badges are served by Cloudflare Workers, operated by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Data may be transferred to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For troubleshooting, logs of requests to the worker (including the requested URL, status code, error messages and technical connection data) are stored at Cloudflare for no longer than 3 days. More information: Cloudflare's privacy policy.

4. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and loads no content from third parties (including no external fonts). Settings in the badge builder are not stored.

5. Live badges

For live badges our server fetches public data from GitHub, npm and Codecov (for example the star count of a repository). No data about visitors is passed on to these services.
